COBALITE / PHRASE ECOLOGY

Play first. Capture what happens. Transform without losing the original.

Start at low speaker volume.

Transport

Ready · capture begins on the first note.
Loop length is rounded up to a four-beat bar, with a 32-bar maximum. Tempo can change without changing note intervals in beats.

Play

Use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, or a MIDI keyboard. Capture again to close the loop. Play over it without changing the recording.

Transform the phrase

Edits take effect at the next loop boundary, with note-offs preserved. Rotation moves the whole pattern; Harmony uses only pitches already in the capture. Variation changes the choice of added or omitted notes per loop. Swing is scaled by Quantize. The original recording is never overwritten.
