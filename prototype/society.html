COBALITE / VOICE SOCIETY

Eight voices with individual identities. Chords remembered as something you played, not a preset name.

Start with low volume.

Voice population

Green: voice amplitude. Gold: retained playing history. Each slot has its own colour, phase and envelope response.

Individuality controls differences between voices; Cohesion controls shared vs individual movement; History makes repeatedly allocated voices warmer. Unison and Stack use the same eight-voice budget.

Chord memory

Memory: 0 +4 +7 semitones
Capture notes together or one by one. Finish, then use one key to transpose the exact voicing. Inversion and Spread preserve pitch classes. New voicing settings apply to the next trigger.

Play

Computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K. Or connect a MIDI keyboard. Chord memory is most useful in Poly; selecting a new voice mode releases the current notes.
